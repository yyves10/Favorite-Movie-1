Movie Summary
Spider-Man: No Way Home is about Peter Parker, whose identity as Spider-Man is revealed to the world. He asks Doctor Strange for help, but a spell goes wrong and brings villains from other universes into his world. Peter must face these enemies and make difficult choices to protect the people he loves.

Why I Like This Movie
I like Spider-Man: No Way Home because it has exciting action scenes, an interesting story, and emotional moments. My favorite part is when the three Spider-Men work together to fight the villains. The movie teaches us about responsibility, friendship, and sacrifice.
